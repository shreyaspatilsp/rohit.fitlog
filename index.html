About Rohit

Built on stage. Coached on the gym floor.

Rohit Mhetre has spent over a decade competing and coaching — as a bodybuilder, a personal trainer, and co-founder of RoyalX Gymnasium & Fitness Centre in Miraj.

He works with clients on personal training, fitness coaching, nutrition and diet consultation — one plan at a time, built around where you're starting from.
